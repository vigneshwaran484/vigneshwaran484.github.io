Privacy policy

Linux Calendar Sync is a personal tool used only by its author to sync their own Google Calendar with a calendar stored on their own computer.

  • Data accessed: calendar events of the signed-in Google account (Google Calendar scope), only to copy them between Google and the local computer.
  • Where it is stored: only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, and no data is sold, shared or used for any other purpose.
  • Access tokens: kept locally on that computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
